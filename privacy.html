Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Twinhaven is an independent project that is preparing its first release. Contact: twinhaven.hello@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we collect your email address and the optional answers you give (your role, main CAD, SolidWorks version, interest in the private beta).

Why

To email you when Twinhaven is ready to try and, if you asked, to invite you to the private beta. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not send marketing beyond that.

Where it is stored

Submissions are stored by our form provider, Formspree (formspree.io), on our behalf. This site uses no cookies and no advertising. To count visits we use Cloudflare Web Analytics (cloudflare.com): it does not set cookies, does not use local storage and does not track you across sites; it records only page views, the referring page, country and device type, without identifying you.

How long

Until the public launch plus 12 months, or until you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to twinhaven.hello@gmail.com. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
